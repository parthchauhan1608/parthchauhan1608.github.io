Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026

Snake Trap ("the app") is made by Pixelora Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

Game data. Your level progress, stars, coins, boosters, daily reward and award status, and the display name you choose. It is stored on your device and, so you don't lose it, in our cloud database (Google Firebase).

Account data. When you open the app you get an anonymous account, identified by a random ID. If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your Google account's email address and name through Firebase Authentication, to link your progress to that account.

Leaderboard. Your display name, avatar, frame, best level and the number of levels you cleared today are shown to other players on the in-game leaderboard. You can change your display name in Profile at any time. The leaderboard also includes computer-controlled AI rivals.

Usage and diagnostics. Google Analytics for Firebase records how the app is used (for example levels started and completed, boosters used, ads watched), and Firebase Crashlytics records crash reports and diagnostics, with device model, OS version and app version.

Advertising. The app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (from which approximate location can be inferred) and information about ad interactions, to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first, and you can change it at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, files or audio.

Why we use it

To run the game and save your progress; to show the leaderboard; to fix crashes and improve the game; and to show ads that keep the game free.

Who we share it with

Only with the service providers that run parts of the app, under their own terms: Google Firebase (hosting, sign-in, analytics, crash reports) and Google AdMob (ads). Your leaderboard profile is visible to other players. We do not sell your personal information.

  • Google: policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data from apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

How long we keep it

Game data and your leaderboard profile are kept while your account exists. Analytics and crash data are kept for the periods set by Google (analytics data retention: 14 months).

Deleting your data

You can delete your account, cloud save and leaderboard profile at any time in the app (Profile → Delete account), or ask us to do it: see Delete your account. Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your device.

Children

Snake Trap is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We will update this page when the app changes how it uses data, and change the date above.
