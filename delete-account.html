Delete your Snake Trap account

Snake Trap by {{STUDIO_NAME}}. You can delete your account and data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Snake Trap.
  2. Tap Profile in the bottom bar.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm. If you signed in with Google, Google asks you to confirm it's you.

By email (if you no longer have the app)

Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with the subject "Delete my Snake Trap account" and the Google account email you signed in with (or your display name if you played as a guest). We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your cloud save (levels, stars, coins, boosters, cosmetics, rewards).
  • Your sign-in account and your leaderboard profile (name, avatar, best level).

What is kept

  • Anonymous analytics and crash reports kept by Google for up to 14 months; they are not linked to your name or email.

Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your phone, but not your cloud save; use the steps above for that.
